## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our project seriously and appreciate any security vulnerabilities reported to us. If you have discovered a security vulnerability in our project, please follow these steps to report it:

1. Do not publish the report publicly on forums, issues, or any other public channels before communicating it to us.
2. Send a detailed vulnerability report to our security email: security@fractalgamestudios.com
3. Describe the issue in detail, providing everything necessary to reproduce the problem. This may include:
   - Type of vulnerability (e.g., buffer overflow, XSS, etc.)
   - Steps to reproduce
   - The potential impact of the vulnerability
4. Allow us to respond to your report within 24-48 hours to acknowledge receipt and coordinate with you for responsible disclosure and remediation of the issue.

We commit to working with you to understand the issue quickly and to resolve the vulnerability according to best security practices and in constant communication with you.

## Acknowledgements
